# VegaKart Customer Support

## About VegaKart

VegaKart is an Indian online store delivering across India. It sells electronics, fashion, groceries and household essentials, and aims for fast, reliable and affordable delivery.

## Customer support hours

The VegaKart customer support team is available 7 days a week, from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST. The chatbot is available at any time, and complaints filed outside these hours are picked up when the team is back.

## Contact channels

Besides the chatbot, you can contact VegaKart by email at support@vegakart.in, by phone on 1800-123-VEGA (8342), which is toll-free, or through live chat on the website during support hours.

## How to file a complaint

To file a complaint, give the chatbot your full name, a 10-digit Indian mobile number, your email address and a description of the problem. You do not need to log in. Once the complaint is filed you receive a unique complaint ID as confirmation.

## Complaint ID and tracking

Use your complaint ID to check the details and status of your complaint at any time through the chatbot. The complaint ID is unique but not private: anyone who has it can see the complaint details, so keep it safe. Complaints cannot be changed after filing through the chatbot; email support@vegakart.in with your complaint ID for corrections.

## Complaint resolution time

Simple complaints, such as a delivery delay or a refund status question, are usually resolved within 24–48 hours. More complex issues can take up to 5 business days.

## Complaint escalation

If a complaint is not resolved within 3 business days, it is escalated automatically to a senior support executive. You can also ask to escalate in the chat for priority handling.

## Types of issues you can report

You can file a complaint about damaged or defective items, delayed delivery, a wrong product, a refund not received, a payment deducted without an order confirmation, or a technical problem with the website or app.

## Data privacy

VegaKart stores complaint data securely and uses it only to resolve your issue. Your information is never shared with third-party advertisers.
