# VegaKart Refunds Policy

## Refund timeline after a return

Once a returned item is picked up and passes the quality check, the refund is initiated within 48 hours. It then takes up to 5 business days to reflect in your account, depending on your bank. UPI and wallet refunds are usually faster, often within 1–2 business days.

## Where the refund goes (prepaid orders)

For orders paid online by UPI, debit card, credit card, net banking or wallet, the refund goes back to the same payment method used for the order. VegaKart cannot send it to a different account or card.

## Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, you can choose where the refund goes: your bank account by NEFT, or your VegaKart Wallet. Wallet refunds are instant once initiated. Bank refunds take up to 5 business days, and you must enter your account number and IFSC code in My Orders.

## Payment deducted but no order confirmed

If money was deducted from your account but no order was confirmed, the payment is reversed automatically within 5 business days. You do not need to raise a request. If it has not come back after 5 business days, file a complaint with your transaction ID and the date of the payment.

## Partial refunds

If you return only some items from an order with several items, only the returned items are refunded. Delivery charges and COD fees are not refunded unless the whole order is returned because it was damaged, defective or wrong.

## Refunds that need approval

Any refund above ₹1,000 must be approved by a senior support executive before it is processed. The VegaKart chatbot cannot approve these refunds itself; it will pass the request to a senior executive, who reviews it within 2 business days. Refunds of ₹1,000 or less are processed automatically once the return is accepted.

## Refund not received

If your refund has not reached you after 5 business days from initiation, first check your bank or UPI statement for a credit from VegaKart. If it is not there, file a complaint with your order ID, and the support team will share the bank reference number so you can trace it with your bank.
