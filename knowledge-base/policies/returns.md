# VegaKart Returns Policy

## Standard return window

Most items bought on VegaKart can be returned within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused, with all tags attached and in its original packaging, including any free gifts that came with it. Some categories have different rules, listed below.

## How to request a return

Open My Orders in the VegaKart app or website, select the item and tap "Return". Choose a reason and, if asked, upload photos. You can also ask the VegaKart chatbot to help. Once the return is approved, a pickup is scheduled automatically. You do not need to ship the item yourself.

## Return pickup

Pickup happens within 1–2 business days of the return request. The delivery partner makes 2 pickup attempts. If both attempts fail, the return request is closed and you will need to raise a new one, as long as you are still within the return window. The partner checks the item at your door, so keep it packed with its tags and accessories.

## Fashion and footwear returns

Clothing, footwear and accessories can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused, unwashed and with tags attached. Each order also gets one free size exchange: request an exchange instead of a return, and the new size is delivered when the old one is picked up.

## Mobile and electronics returns

Mobiles, laptops, tablets, headphones and other electronics can be returned within 7 days of delivery only if they are defective, damaged or not the item you ordered. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted for electronics. The serial number or IMEI on the returned item must match the one on the invoice, and the item must include its box, charger and all accessories.

## Large appliance returns

Large appliances such as TVs, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners can be returned within 10 days of delivery, but only as a replacement, not a refund. A technician from the brand inspects the appliance first, usually within 2 business days, and the replacement is approved only if the technician confirms a defect or damage.

## Grocery and perishable items

Groceries, fresh produce, dairy and other perishable items cannot be returned. If an item arrives damaged, expired or is missing from your order, report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, and VegaKart will refund or replace that item.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items can never be returned, even within 7 days: innerwear and lingerie, opened cosmetics and personal care products, personalised or custom-made items, gift cards, and digital products such as software keys. They can only be replaced if they arrive damaged or wrong.

## Damaged or wrong item received

If any item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the item and the packaging. This applies to every category, including non-returnable items. VegaKart will arrange a free replacement or a refund.
