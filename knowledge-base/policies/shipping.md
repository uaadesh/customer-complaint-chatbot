# VegaKart Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Standard delivery time

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days from the order date across most of India. The expected delivery date is shown before you pay and in My Orders.

## Express delivery

Express delivery brings your order in 1–2 days in metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad and Pune) for an extra ₹99. It is available only on items marked "Express" and only for orders placed before 2 PM.

## Delivery to remote areas

Orders to remote areas, including the North-East states, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, and the Andaman and Nicobar and Lakshadweep islands, take 7–10 business days. Express delivery is not available there.

## Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 have a delivery charge of ₹40. Express delivery costs ₹99 extra, whatever the order value.

## Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on orders up to ₹10,000. A COD fee of ₹20 is added to each COD order. You can pay the delivery partner in cash or by scanning their UPI QR code.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed in My Orders only before the order is dispatched. After dispatch the address cannot be changed; you can refuse the delivery and place a new order with the right address.

## Failed delivery attempts

The delivery partner makes 3 delivery attempts on different days and calls before each one. If all 3 attempts fail, the order is returned to the seller and cancelled, and prepaid orders are refunded within 5 business days.

## Delivery delays

Deliveries can be delayed by heavy rain, floods, festivals, strikes or other events outside our control. VegaKart sends an SMS and an app notification with the new expected date. If your order is more than 3 days past its expected date, you can file a complaint about the delay.
