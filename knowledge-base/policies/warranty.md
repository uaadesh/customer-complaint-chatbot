# VegaKart Warranty Policy

## Manufacturer warranty

Products sold on VegaKart carry the manufacturer's warranty shown on the product page. Repairs under warranty are done by the brand's authorised service centres, not by VegaKart. Contact the brand's customer care with your invoice to book a repair.

## Dead on arrival products

If an electronic product or appliance does not work at all when it arrives (dead on arrival), report it within 7 days of delivery. After the brand confirms the problem, usually through a technician visit or a phone check, VegaKart replaces the product free of charge.

## Downloading your invoice

You need the invoice for any warranty claim. Download it from My Orders by selecting the order and tapping "Download invoice". The invoice shows the serial number or IMEI of the product.

## VegaCare extended warranty

VegaCare adds 1 extra year of warranty after the manufacturer's warranty ends. It can be bought for electronics and appliances only, within 15 days of delivery, from My Orders. VegaCare repairs are booked through the VegaKart app, and the plan cannot be cancelled or refunded once bought.

## What the warranty does not cover

Neither the manufacturer warranty nor VegaCare covers physical damage, liquid damage, damage from wrong installation or power surges, or products repaired by an unauthorised person. Accessories such as cables and remotes are covered only if the brand says so.
