# VegaKart Cancellations Policy

## Cancelling before dispatch

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. Open My Orders and tap "Cancel", or ask the VegaKart chatbot. You can cancel one item from an order with several items without cancelling the rest.

## Cancelling after dispatch

Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery when the delivery partner arrives, and the order is then returned and cancelled. Alternatively, accept it and request a return, if the item is returnable.

## Grocery slot cancellations

Grocery orders with a delivery slot can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the slot starts. After that the order is already packed and cannot be cancelled.

## Refund for a cancelled order

For prepaid orders, the full amount, including delivery charges, is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days of the cancellation. Cash on Delivery orders need no refund, because nothing was paid.

## Cancellation by the seller

Sometimes a seller cancels an order, for example because the item is out of stock or cannot be delivered to your pincode. You are notified by SMS and email, and prepaid orders get a full refund automatically within 5 business days. You do not need to raise a request.
